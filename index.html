About the Game

Backrooms Shooter is a free online 3D third-person shooter set in a maze-like abandoned hotel overrun by Skibidi Toilets. Wake up alone after your squad is gone, shoot your way through the backrooms-style corridors, and escape before the enemies catch up with you.

Clear each section to level up, unlock new weapons, and face tougher enemy waves. Look out for explosive barrels and green exit signs — they are your best friends in the maze.

How to Play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, and fire with a left click. Right-click aims more precisely, Space jumps, Left Shift runs, R reloads, G throws a grenade, and V swings a melee attack.

Use the mouse scroll to switch between unlocked weapons. Follow the green exit signs to find your way out of the hotel maze and level up.

Explore the Hotel

The backrooms hotel is a genuine maze of identical-looking corridors. Keep an eye out for exit signs as you clear rooms, and check every corner — useful items and hidden paths are easy to miss.

Weapons, Barrels, and Waves

Shoot explosive barrels near groups of enemies to take out several at once. Cycle between shotguns, rifles, and melee attacks depending on how many enemies are closing in and how much ammo you have left. Every cleared section unlocks new weapons and harder waves.

Play on Mobile or Desktop

Backrooms Shooter runs directly in your browser on supported desktop and mobile devices. For the best experience, use an up-to-date browser. If the game does not load, refresh the page or check your connection.
